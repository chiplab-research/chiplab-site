Pre-tapeout bug hunt

We look for functional bugs in your RTL before you tape out. You pay only for bugs that are verified.

The engineering is done by AI coding agents (Claude and Codex), directed by ChipLab's founder, Nathan Chandrasekar. One agent finds and documents each bug. A different agent reproduces it independently before it reaches you.

What each bug report contains

  • A minimal reproducer that runs on your RTL.
  • A trace against a golden model or the specification, showing where the design diverges.
  • The root cause in your RTL.
  • A check against your known-issues list, so you are not sent what you already know.

Results are private to you. We do not publish client findings or file them anywhere.

How it works

  1. Scope

    You send the RTL, the spec or design notes, and your known-issues list. We confirm the design is eligible, screen you against the US Consolidated Screening List, and agree rates and a cap in writing.

  2. Hunt

    Agents write tests and compare your design with a golden model or the spec, using open-source simulators (Verilator and Icarus Verilog).

  3. Independent reproduction

    A second agent reproduces each candidate bug from scratch. A bug it cannot reproduce is not reported.

  4. Report

    You receive each verified bug privately. You pay per verified bug, up to the cap.

  5. Delete

    At the end of the engagement we delete your files.

Pilots

We are running a small number of pre-tapeout pilots with professional teams, on published or EAR99 designs. If your design has a tapeout date coming up, email agents@donmakino.com with a link to the RTL and the spec you want it checked against.

Terms

  • To start, we take only published designs or EAR99 designs. We screen every client against the US Consolidated Screening List.
  • RTL only. We do not take PDK files or NDA'd libraries.
  • Your RTL is processed by the commercial AI models our agents run on, on plans where your data is not used for model training. We name each provider in the engagement letter.
  • Your files are deleted at the end of the engagement.
  • This is not sign-off and not a warranty that your RTL is free of bugs.
